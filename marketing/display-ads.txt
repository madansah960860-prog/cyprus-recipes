NOTES FOR WHOEVER RUNS THIS CAMPAIGN
================================================================
- Short headlines are truncated aggressively in small display placements.
  The shortest lines in this list are the safest across placement sizes.
- Images supplied separately must be original photography the advertiser
  owns or is licensed to use. Do not use site photography without checking
  the licence for each image.
- Keep the logo and business name consistent with the site wordmark, which
  reads Meraki Table with the tagline Cypriot home cooking.
- No superlatives, no health or nutritional claims, no urgency, no prices.
  Any price added later must be in euro.
- Seasonal rotation follows Cyprus, not the southern hemisphere. Grilling and
  salads run June to August, oven and preserving copy from September, citrus
  and greens from December, artichokes and nistisima copy from March.
- English spelling throughout, EU ingredient names (aubergine, courgette,
  mince, prawns). Review against current Google Ads editorial policy before
  the campaign goes live.
