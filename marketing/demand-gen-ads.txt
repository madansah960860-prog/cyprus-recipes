NOTES FOR WHOEVER RUNS THIS CAMPAIGN
================================================================
- Every line is written to stand alone, because Google mixes assets.
- No superlatives, no health or nutritional claims, no urgency, no prices.
- No all-caps words, no repeated punctuation, no emoji, no phone numbers.
- Angles are varied on purpose: speed, seasonality, budget, skill level,
  family cooking, meze and entertaining, fasting periods and dietary options.
- Seasonal assets follow the Cypriot year. Grill and salad copy runs June to
  August, slow cooking and preserving from September, citrus and greens from
  December, artichokes and nistisima copy from March.
- Halloumi, souvla, meze, laiki and nistisima read as everyday words to a
  Cypriot audience. For EU markets outside Cyprus, prefer the assets that do
  not depend on them.
- Landing page should match the asset that led to it. Meal plan copy should
  point at the meal plans page, not the home page.
- Any pricing ever added must be in euro and must match the site.
