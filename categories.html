Every collection on the site

Four collections sorted by how you are cooking tonight, and three sorted by where the food comes from. Most recipes sit in two of them, because a platter of charred vegetables is both a fire dish and a meat-free one.

By occasion

Start here if the question is how much time you have and how many people are coming.

  • Quick Weeknight Dinners

    Five complete recipes, all on the table in forty minutes, with one pan and one board. Rice bowls, a pasta salad, eggs, grilled salmon and the bruschetta you make when the tomatoes are right.

  • Baking & Desserts

    Seven complete recipes weighed in grams, with a fan-forced temperature beside every conventional one, set next to the island sweets they share a table with: flaounes, daktyla, loukoumades and spoon sweets.

  • BBQ & Outdoor

    Charcoal, the foukou and a Sunday that runs to five hours. Five complete recipes including a whole fish, a pizza with the lid down, and the vegetable platter that saves the vegetarians.

  • Vegetarian & Vegan

    Cyprus has cooked without animal products for centuries and called it nistisima. Six complete recipes with the vegan swap written out rather than left to you, plus the pulses and wild greens behind them.

By cuisine

Three ways of cooking that a Cypriot kitchen moves between in the same week without thinking about it.

  • Modern Cypriot

    Village recipes cooked in city kitchens. Halloumi and anari, meze as a way of eating rather than a menu section, charcoal, wild greens and what the laiki has this week.

  • Asian at Home

    A twelve-item pantry list that unlocks most weeknight cooking, where to find it in Limassol and Nicosia, and honest notes about what we are and are not qualified to write.

  • Mediterranean

    Everywhere else on the same sea. Italian, Spanish, Levantine and North African cooking, and the five jars that redirect ingredients you were buying anyway.

How to pick one

If you do not know where to start, work backwards from the clock and the calendar rather than from a craving. It is a faster route to dinner and it wastes less.

Other ways in

Collections are only one route through the site. If you would rather search by ingredient, filter by time or dietary need, or just see everything at once, the full index does all of that on one page.

If you are cooking for the week rather than for tonight, the meal plans group five dinners around a single shop, with the list already written and grouped by aisle, plus a note on what to buy at the laiki instead of the supermarket.

Reference pages

Two pages that are not recipes and get used constantly.

  • Kitchen basics and conversions, including the metric 250 ml cup, the difference between it and the 240 ml US cup, and oven temperature charts with fan-forced and gas mark columns.
  • Seasonal produce guide, written for the Cypriot growing calendar, where summer runs from June to August and the artichokes arrive in March.
