Requests about your personal data are handled under the GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and Cyprus Law 125(I)/2018. We answer access, correction, erasure, portability and objection requests within one month, and we will tell you if a complicated request needs longer. The privacy policy explains how to complain to us, and how to complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Nicosia if our answer does not satisfy you.

Consumer questions

If you bought a cookbook, a meal plan bundle or a membership from us, your rights under the Consumer Protection Law of Cyprus and EU consumer law are unaffected by anything on this site. Write to hello@merakitable.com.cy first and we will sort it out. You can also contact the Consumer Protection Service of the Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry.
